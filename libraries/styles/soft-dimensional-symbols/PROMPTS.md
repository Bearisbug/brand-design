# 轻立体图形 · Prompt

依据 [STYLE.md](STYLE.md)选路线，提交前替换全部字段。以下是依据参考编写的自然语言模板，未实测；不是原作者提示词。参考必须明确承担构思、材质或呈现角色，用户已确定的品牌输入与本轮范围优先。

## 几何与字母

```text
Design an original brand mark for {{brand}}, a product for {{purpose}}.
Build its identity from {{one geometric relationship or exact letterform}}.
Create a distinctive silhouette with {{key curves, gaps or structural turns}}.
Add shallow depth through {{layer overlap, folded planes or rounded edge thickness}}.
Use {{brand palette}} with {{gradient direction and contrast}}, soft broad highlights
and restrained contact shadows. Keep the main shape readable without these effects.
Canvas occupancy and placement: {{scale and position}}.
Exact text: {{text instruction}}. Presentation: {{required view and background}}.
The references establish construction and surface treatment, not reusable geometry.
Do not copy their letter outlines, element arrangements or brand-specific symbols.
```

检查轮廓是否有独立识别、负空间是否清楚、字母是否正确。渐变或表面变化不能冒充新的构思。

## 圆润角色

```text
Create an original character-based brand mark for {{brand}}, which helps people
{{purpose}}. Derive the character's body from {{product-relevant form}}.
Keep one memorable rounded silhouette with {{distinctive structural detail}}.
Use {{minimal facial features}} to express {{desired personality}}.
Render it with {{flat, shallow layered or softly rounded treatment}},
{{brand palette}}, broad gentle highlights and {{shadow treatment}}.
Keep accessories limited to {{necessary theme element or none}}.
Make the expression and silhouette readable at {{target size}}.
Presentation: {{canvas, position and background}}. Exact text: {{text or none}}.
Invent the body and facial arrangement for this brand rather than adapting a
reference mascot's existing outline.
```

先比较轮廓与表情，再比较材质；禁止仅靠给参考换颜色、换嘴形或换配件交付新角色。

## 定向修改

```text
Use {{approved own-brand version}} as the shape reference.
Preserve {{silhouette, counters, arrangement, facial features and exact lettering}}.
Change only {{one requested dimension}} toward {{observable target}}.
Return {{required comparison view}} while retaining the approved brand identity.
```

用户要求换构思时，不用该模板锁定旧轮廓。生成后必须实际对照形状、文字与目标尺寸；不能因提示词写了保持就判定保持成功。
